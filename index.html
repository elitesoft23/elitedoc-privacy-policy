The EliteDoc Privacy Guarantee

EliteDoc does NOT collect, upload, transmit, sell, monitor, or share your documents, photos, biometric representations, signatures, or device metadata. Everything processed inside EliteDoc stays in your personal possession at all times.

01 Introduction & Core Philosophy

Welcome to EliteDoc: PDF Scanner & Editor ("EliteDoc", "we", "our", or "the App"), engineered and maintained by elitesoft23.

We believe user privacy is a fundamental human right. Unlike legacy scanning apps that upload sensitive invoices, tax returns, contracts, and passports to remote cloud servers for monetization or AI training, EliteDoc is purpose-built as an offline-first on-device utility.

When you scan or edit a file in EliteDoc, processing occurs directly on your device's local CPU and GPU. At no point is your data sent across the internet to our servers or any third-party infrastructure.

02 Information We Do NOT Collect

Because our architecture has no backend server or cloud sync system, we do not and cannot collect the following:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We never ask for your name, phone number, home address, social security number, or login credentials.
  • No Scanned Document Contents: Text extracted via Optical Character Recognition (OCR), scanned pages, invoices, ID cards, and receipts are never read or stored externally.
  • No Biometric Data or Facial Vectors: In our Passport & Photo Studio, facial framing guides are calculated purely as mathematical on-screen viewport ratios. No biometric templates, facial recognition profiles, or vector embeddings are extracted or shared.
  • No Location Data: EliteDoc does not request GPS, network location, or coarse location coordinates.
  • No Analytics or Behavioral Profiles: We do not integrate tracking SDKs such as Google Firebase Analytics, Facebook Analytics, Adjust, or AppsFlyer.

03 Device Permissions & Transparent Usage

EliteDoc requests only the bare minimum operating system permissions strictly required for native camera scanning and file management:

Permission System Identifier Why It Is Needed Privacy Handling
Camera android.permission.CAMERA To capture document scans, receipts, QR codes, and passport portraits. Frames are processed in local volatile RAM only. The video feed is never transmitted or broadcasted.
Read Media / Images READ_MEDIA_IMAGES To allow you to pick and import images from your gallery to edit or convert to PDF. Only the specific photo(s) selected by you in the system picker are accessed.
Device Storage WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (API ≤ 29) To export your finished PDFs, images, and OCR text to device storage. Files are written directly into your device's public storage folder.

04 On-Device Processing & Offline AI

EliteDoc utilizes advanced offline algorithms and on-device machine learning models:

4.1 Offline Optical Character Recognition (OCR)

Text recognition is performed entirely on your device via Google ML Kit On-Device Vision models. Text extraction executes locally on your mobile processor without sending any images or extracted strings over the network.

4.2 On-Device AI Background Segmentation

Our passport photo background isolation is powered by Google ML Kit Selfie Segmentation running 100% offline. The neural network computes subject masks locally on your hardware. No portraits or personal likenesses are uploaded.

4.3 Cryptographic Signatures & PDF Encryption

Digital signatures, cryptographic certificates (X.509), and 128-bit/256-bit ISO 32000-1 PDF encryption are calculated purely in device memory. Passwords are never logged or stored in plain text.

05 Unified Storage: The EliteDoc Folder Policy

To protect user convenience and ensure you always know where your private documents reside, EliteDoc adheres to a transparent, unified file storage policy:

📂

Internal Storage / EliteDoc

All exported files (PDF documents, high-res photos, text notes, and signatures) are placed into a single unified directory on your device: /storage/emulated/0/EliteDoc.

We do not scatter your files across obscure private cache directories or separate folders. You can access, backup, copy, or permanently delete your files at any time using your preferred Android File Manager.

06 Third-Party Libraries & Zero-Advertising Policy

EliteDoc has no advertising banners, no interstitial video ads, and no data monetization partners.

The open-source components used by the app (such as the Flutter framework, printing drivers, and image encoders) are strictly functional runtime libraries that execute locally. We do not incorporate any SDKs that transmit telemetry, tracking cookies, or advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID).

07 Data Retention, Control & Complete Erasure

You are the sole custodian of your data. Because no information is held on remote servers, managing your data is straightforward:

  • Delete Specific Documents: Open EliteDoc > Document Vault > swipe or select any document and tap Delete.
  • Clear Temporary Cache: Open EliteDoc > Settings > Storage & Cache > tap "Clear Cache & Temporary Files".
  • Total Erasure: Uninstalling the EliteDoc application from your Android device settings immediately and permanently deletes all internal sandboxed data.

08 Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

EliteDoc does not target, market to, or solicit information from children under the age of 13. Furthermore, because EliteDoc collects zero personal data from any user, we comply fully with the requirements of the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.

09 Global Privacy Compliance (GDPR, CCPA & CalOPPA)

Although we do not collect personal data, we support and uphold global privacy frameworks:

  • GDPR (European Union): Under the General Data Protection Regulation, principles of data minimization and privacy-by-design are fundamental. EliteDoc embodies these by design through 100% on-device processing.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California, USA): We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as defined by California privacy laws, as no personal information is collected or monetized.
  • Google Play Store Developer Policies: This policy conforms strictly with Google Play's User Data and Device Permissions guidelines.
